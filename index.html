The research

We don't guess
what to post. We check.

Every week we look at what local businesses post and compare each reel with that account's own usual views. When one breaks out, we take it apart beat by beat. These six things decide most of it.

01

The hook

What's on screen in the first second. Movement, a face or a half-typed search beats a logo every time.

02

The people

Real staff and regulars, filmed on a phone, usually beat polished product shots.

03

The payoff

The pour, the bite, the reveal. Where it lands, and whether the best shot comes early enough.

04

The length

Most winners are short. We cut to the moment and leave the process shots out.

05

The sound

Real sound from the place, or licensed music. Never something you're not allowed to use.

06

The reason

Every post has a job: an event, an offer, a question, something new. "Here's our product" isn't one.

Questions

The things owners ask.

What does the free concept cost?+

Nothing. We make a website concept, a 15-second reel and nine posts for your business. If you like it, we'll talk about making it real. If you don't, you've lost nothing.

Do I have to film anything?+

Not for the concept. For the real thing, we send a one-page shot list and it takes about twenty minutes on a phone. You never have to come up with ideas for what to film.

Who posts it?+

Whoever does now. Everything arrives in a numbered folder, in posting order, with a captions doc to match. Nothing goes on your accounts without your yes.

Do you use AI?+

Yes, as a tool for the making: animation, motion, sound. But your food, drinks, rooms and people are your real photos. If anything is illustrative, it's labelled on screen and swapped for the real thing before it goes live.

Can I use the music on my concept reel?+

No. Any music on a concept isn't ours, so it isn't ours to hand over. When you go ahead, we swap it for something you're allowed to use, such as a track from the platform's own library.

Why is my concept watermarked?+

So it can't be screenshotted, posted or passed around before you've said yes. Once you go ahead, you get clean files with no watermark.

How much does it cost after that?+

It depends on what you want: a one-off website, weekly content, or both. You'll get a clear price once you've seen your concept and know whether you want it.

Where are you?+

Leeds and the North East. We work with independents: pubs, cafés and coffee shops, bakeries, bars and hotels.
