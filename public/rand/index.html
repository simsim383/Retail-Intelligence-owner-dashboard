04

Two things I noticed.

The menu

Your drinks aren't online yet.

I couldn't find your café menu or any prices on your website, on Google or on the delivery apps. People search for the drink first, so a menu with photos and prices is the quickest win.

Opening hours

Two sets of hours.

Your website says all three shops open 7am–6pm every day. Google shows something different (checked in November):

Roundhay7am–6pm · Sun from 7:30am
University8am–5pm · Sun from 9am
City Centre8am–4pm weekdays · closed weekends

It's worth checking which is right, because customers trust Google.
